<product> film — ledger
=======================

Every number was printed by a script (build.py runs them all) or by the named
reviewer. A round's numbers are from the build that round reviewed.

Round <n> — <self-check | reviewer, verdict> (<date>)
- Media: <w>x<h>, <fps> fps, <s> s, H.264 yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz, faststart; <MB>.
- Contrast: <pair ratio, ...>; minimum 4.5.
- Loudness: mix <before LUFS/dBTP> -> <after>; music alone <before> -> <after>.
- Sync: <n> events, worst <ms> (limit one frame); negative control <ms, exit 1>.
- Facts: <n> lines, <n> used, <failures>.
- Muted test: <beats the viewer got; beats missed>.
- Problems fixed, worst first: <problem -> fix>.
